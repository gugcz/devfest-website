How to get to Uhelný Mlýn, DevFest.cz 2026 — Venue

Uhelný Mlýn is a converted industrial mill in Libčice nad Vltavou, on the river just north of Prague.

Address

Areál Šroubáren 860

252 66 Libčice nad Vltavou

Registration opened at 08:00, the keynote started at 09:00.

Open in mapsStation to venue

The route

A direct train, 25 minutes from the city centre, then a ten-minute walk.

  1. 01

    Take the train from Prague

    Take the S4 toward Kralupy nad Vltavou from Praha-Bubny. Trains leave about every 30 minutes.

    Get off
    Libčice nad Vltavou
    Ride
    25 min
  2. 02

    Walk to Uhelný Mlýn

    From Libčice nad Vltavou station it is a ten-minute walk, about 0.7 km, to the entrance in the black metal building.

    Walk
    10 min
    Distance
    0.7 km
    Station to venue

Coming by car

There is only a limited number of parking places, at the far end of the complex. Follow the signs, or better, share a ride or take the train.

Parking
Limited
EV chargers
None on site

The entrance

Red-brick halls, one tall chimney, and the doors in the black metal building, up a few concrete steps.
