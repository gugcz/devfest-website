1. Who we are

DevFest.cz 2026 is organised by GUG.cz, z.s. (formerly GDG Czech Republic), a registered association (zapsaný spolek) and the data controller for the personal data described here. For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at devfest@gug.cz.

2. Data we collect and why

a) Newsletter sign-up

When you submit your email address via the notification form, we store it in SmartEmailing (our email service provider) to send you conference updates such as ticket availability announcements and event news. Legal basis: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You may unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.

b) Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics (measurement ID G-L5NK2S2EZ0) to understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve it. It runs only if you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner.

Before you decide, and if you decline: analytics does not run. The site loads no Google Analytics code, sets no analytics cookie, and sends Google no measurement data about your visit.

If you accept: analytics loads and sets the _ga cookies listed below, which give you a random analytics identifier. That lets us count returning visitors and follow sessions across pages — pages visited, session duration, browser type. We never collect your name, email, or any other information that identifies you personally. Legal basis: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time:(also in the footer of every page) deletes the analytics cookies and the installation id, forgets your choice and asks again.

Alongside page views we also record a few interaction events: opening the ticket checkout, completing a newsletter sign-up or a ticket purchase, and submitting a company invoice request. These record the action itself — which ticket wave, its price, how many tickets — and never your name, email, company details, or anything else that identifies you.

With the same consent we also run Google Firebase Performance Monitoring, which measures how fast the site loads and responds for you: page load timings, Core Web Vitals and the duration of requests the page makes, together with browser, device type, connection type and coarse country. It stores a random installation id in your browser (IndexedDB, listed below) and never records page content or anything you type.

The data is used only for analytics — never for advertising, ad personalisation, or profiling.

c) Server logs

Our hosting provider (Firebase Hosting) automatically records standard access logs (IP address, browser, requested URL). These are retained for up to 30 days for security purposes. Legal basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

d) Bot protection (reCAPTCHA)

To protect our ticketing data from automated scraping and abuse, the site uses Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise (via Firebase App Check). reCAPTCHA runs in the background to tell humans from bots, analysing signals such as your interactions with the page, device and browser information, and IP address. This data is shared with Google and processed under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Because this is a security measure, it runs regardless of your cookie choice. Legal basis: legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

e) Company invoice requests

If you request a company invoice via the company invoice form, we collect your company name, registration number (IČO), VAT identification number (DIČ, if provided), billing address (street, city, ZIP, country), contact email, and the number of tickets requested. We use this data to issue the invoice, process your payment, and send you a discount code to claim your tickets on ti.to. The request is stored in Google Firestore, the invoice is issued through iDoklad, and the discount code is created in ti.to. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and compliance with our legal accounting and tax obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)). Issued invoices and related accounting records are retained for up to 10 years, as required by Czech tax and accounting law.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the processors listed below, under data processing agreements and only to the extent necessary to provide the services described:

  • SmartEmailing — newsletter delivery
  • Google Firebase (Hosting, Analytics, Performance Monitoring, Firestore) — website hosting, analytics, performance measurement, and storage of invoice requests
  • Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise (via Firebase App Check) — bot protection
  • iDoklad (Solitea a.s. / Seyfor, idoklad.cz) — issuing company invoices
  • ti.to — ticket sales and discount-code redemption

5. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect prior processing)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Czech Data Protection Authority (uoou.cz)

To exercise these rights, email us at devfest@gug.cz.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website evolves. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced via our newsletter.

You can use the agenda without logging in. Logging in with your ticket is optional and is needed only for networking, the detective game, stickers and for keeping your favourites on your account.

b) Networking: if your badge is scanned

Another attendee who is logged in to the app can scan the QR code on your badge. If they do, the person who scanned it gets your name and company, the same details your badge already shows, and the app keeps them on that person's account. Your email address is never shared. After the conference we email each attendee the list of people they scanned. The entry is deleted when the person who scanned you deletes their account, and you can ask us to remove it earlier (see section 8).

c) Camera, photos and stickers

The app uses the camera to scan a ticket, badge or game QR code. Those camera frames are processed on your device and are not uploaded or stored. On Android, scanning uses Google's ML Kit barcode model, and Google receives its own usage telemetry from ML Kit.

Stickers are the one place where a photo leaves your device. When you take or pick a photo for stickers, the app sends it to the sticker printer service at the venue, run for DevFest by one of the organisers, which passes it to Google Gemini to draw the stickers. The app tells you this before you take the photo. It does not send the printer an extra copy of the original photo to keep, and it does not record the photo in analytics or logs. What the printer service and Google Gemini keep, and for how long, is set by them; deleting your app account does not reach it. Email devfest@gug.cz if you want us to ask the printer service to remove what it holds.

d) Stored only on your device

Whether you finished onboarding, which reminder topics you chose, and an inbox of up to 100 received notifications are kept in the app's storage on your device. They never reach our servers, are not backed up, and are removed when you uninstall the app.

e) Legal basis

The account, ticket verification, personal agenda, networking connections, the detective game, sticker photos and push notifications are processed to provide the app you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Usage analytics, crash reports, device attestation, update checks and backend logs are processed on the basis of our legitimate interests in keeping the app working, secure and useful (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

f) Analytics and crash reports are always on

Unlike the website, the app has no consent switch: usage analytics and crash reporting are always on, and there is no in-app opt-out. They carry no name, email or user id, and they are never used for advertising, ad personalisation or tracking. If you do not want them sent, please do not use the app.

g) Processors

We do not sell app data, do not share it with third parties acting as independent controllers, and do not use it for advertising or tracking. All data is encrypted in transit. It is processed only by:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, App Check / Play Integrity, ML Kit, Cloud Logging) — running the app and its backend
  • Apple (App Attest, push notification delivery, App Store) — device attestation, notifications and app distribution on iOS
  • ti.to — ticket lookup
  • Resend — sending the verification email and the post-conference list of people you scanned
  • Shorebird — app code updates
  • The venue sticker printer service, run for DevFest by one of the organisers, and Google Gemini — turning your photo into stickers and printing them

Your rights from section 5 apply to app data too.

8. Deleting your app account

In the app: go to Profile > Delete account. This immediately deletes your Firebase Authentication account, your user record (name, email, registration reference, favourite sessions and networking connections), your detective game progress and any pending verification codes.

Without the app: email devfest@gug.cz from the email address on your ticket with the subject "Delete my DevFest app account". We delete the same data within 30 days.

What is not deleted: usage analytics, crash reports and App Check signals are not linked to your account, so they cannot be picked out and deleted; they expire on their own (analytics after 14 months, crash reports after 90 days). Data kept only on your device goes away when you uninstall the app. If another attendee scanned your badge, your name and company stay in their list until they delete their account; email devfest@gug.cz and we will remove them. Sticker photos are held by the sticker printer service and Google Gemini, not on your account (see section 7 c). Your ti.to ticket itself is not affected.

The same instructions are on a page of their own at devfest.cz/delete-account.
